Privacy, kept local.

Photo Metadata Inbox is designed so your photo catalog does not leave your device. The static app has no account, analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.

Data stored on your device

When you import a batch, the app stores filenames, relative folder paths, captions, keywords, workflow status, settings, and change history in your browser's IndexedDB. It does not store image bytes. Imported XMP text may be retained so the original can be preserved in exports.

Files and exports

Browsers expose files you explicitly choose. Processing happens in your browser. The app reads sidecar text and, for JPEG files, at most the first 2 MB to find embedded IPTC; it does not retain image bytes. XMP bundles and catalog backups are downloaded directly to the location you choose. If you use direct folder writing, the app asks for folder permission and makes timestamped backups before replacing matching sidecars.

License verification

If you buy or restore a full-line pass, its token is stored in localStorage and sent through this site's rate-limited verification endpoint at most once per day. That endpoint forwards only the token to api.sociobot.in to check validity. The checkout is hosted by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record, under its own transaction privacy terms. We do not receive your photo metadata through license verification.

Your control

Use Catalog controls to export or clear local data. Clearing removes this browser's queue and history. You can also remove the site's storage through browser settings. Contact privacy@sociobot.in with privacy questions.
